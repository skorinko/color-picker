Privacy Policy

Effective 31 October 2026

In short: Color Picker does not collect, share or sell any personal data. It sees a page only when you ask it to pick a color there, and your colors stay in Safari on your own device.

Who we are

Color Picker for Safari is published by Dmitry Skorinko, an individual developer, through the App Store.

What the extension sees

Color Picker asks for no access to your websites. Safari lets it into a page only when you click its toolbar button, choose it in the page menu, or press its keyboard shortcut, and only into that page.

When you pick a color there, it reads the colors of the page’s elements, and takes a picture of the part of the page you can see, so it can show the pixels under the pointer. The picture stays in memory on the page while you pick, and is gone when you stop. It is never saved or sent anywhere. Color Picker doesn’t keep the page’s text and has no access to your browsing history.

What is stored

For each color you pick, the extension stores the color, the time, and the name of the website it came from (such as example.com, not the full address). It also stores the palettes you save, with their names and colors, and your settings, such as the format to copy in. All of this is kept in Safari’s storage for the extension, on your device. The library keeps your last 300 picks; you can delete any of them, or all of them, at any time.

Nothing leaves your device unless you copy or export it yourself. Removing the app removes everything it stored.

Network requests

Color Picker makes no requests to the developer or to any third party. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting code.

Purchases

Color Picker is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and the developer does not receive your name, email address or payment details. Apple’s own privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Children

Color Picker collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
